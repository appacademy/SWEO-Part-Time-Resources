# Making The Part-Time Program More Flexible

We are thrilled to announce significant updates to our Part-Time Program that have been nearly two years in the making!

Starting July 8th, all cohorts will switch to our new schedule.

You'll receive initial communications directly from your instructor, they'll be able to answer any question you might have.

This is a big change, so **no strikes will be issued for attendance** for the first two weeks to give you time to adjust!

So what are we changing?

## Giving you the weekend back 🥳

### Old 🔴

### Monday - Thursday & Saturday

### New 🟢

### Monday - Friday

## Study when you want ⏰

### Old 🔴

- Mandatory to attend 3 hour class
- You must attend your assigned cohort's timeslot
- 1 Flex Day per week
- Lecture recordings only available if you were absent

#### Schedule

Monday - Thursday

- Access to Instructors if you're in the EST Cohort

  - 6pm - 9pm EST / 3pm - 6pm PST

  - Office Hours for 30m after class

- Access to Instructors if you're in the EST Cohort

  - 10pm - 1am EST / 7pm - 10pm PST

  - Office Hours for 30m after class

Saturday

- Access to Instructors for everyone

  - 12pm - 6:30pm EST / 9am - 3:30pm PST

  - Office Hours for 1 hour after class

### New 🟢

- Mandatory to attend 1 hour lecture
- You're free to attend either lecture slot, or both!
- Access to Instructors for the rest of the day
- 1 Flex Day per week
- All lectures recordings always available

#### Schedule

Monday - Friday

- Lecture Option 1

  - 8pm - 9pm EST / 5pm - 6pm PST

- Lecture Option 2

  - 10pm - 11pm EST / 7pm - 8pm PST

- Access to Instructors

  - 5:30pm - 12:30am EST / 2:30pm - 10:30pm PST

## Study how you want 📚

### Old 🔴

- Lecture: 1 hour

  - Mandatory
  - Focused on this week's content

- Forced pair programming: 2 hours
  - Mandatory
  - Focused on this week's content

### New 🟢

- Open Office Hours: 3 options, 1 - 2 hours each

  - Optional
  - Make study groups, or work on your own
  - Focused on whatever content you want to work on

- Lecture: 1 hour
  - Mandatory
  - Focused on this week's content

This is an additional 20 hours per week of support 🎉

## Say goodbye to test anxiety 😮‍💨

### Old 🔴

- In-class assessments every other Monday
- 80% required to pass
- 1 attempt
- 3 hour time limit (or less)

### New 🟢

- Take-home assessments every other Friday
- 80% required to pass
- Unlimited attempts
- Due by 11:59pm PST on Sunday

## Changes go live July 8th 🚀

See you then, programmers!
